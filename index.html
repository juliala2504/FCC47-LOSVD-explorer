How to read this page

  • The left panel shows the projected quantity of the selected data set on the spatial bins of the PowerBin tessellation; the dashed circle marks the adopted NSC effective radius, Reff = 0.75″.
  • The right panel shows the posterior LOSVD of the selected bin: the solid line is the posterior median, the shaded band the 16th–84th percentile credible interval, and the dashed vertical line the median velocity of that bin.
  • With Overlay matched bin the LOSVD of the spatially nearest bin of the other data set is drawn on top, the same nearest-neighbour matching used for the representative LOSVDs in the paper. Because the two runs use different velocity sampling (Δv = 30 and 45 km s⁻¹), overlaid profiles are shown as densities normalised to unit area, ∫f(v) dv = 1.
  • Velocities are relative to the systemic velocity. S/N is the effective S/Nreal measured a posteriori from the fit residuals.

Data

Products are the posterior summaries of the adopted Bayes-LOSVD runs; the page loads them directly as JSON and renders everything in the browser, with no server-side computation.
